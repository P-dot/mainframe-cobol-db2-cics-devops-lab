LAB 18 - ADCD LOADDB STARTUP VERIFICATION FOR DB2 AND CICS

OBJECTIVE:
  Verify the current ADCD startup profile and confirm DB2/CICS availability safely.

KEY FINDINGS:
  USED LOADDB IN SYS1.IPLPARM ON 0A82
  IEASYS LIST = DB
  IPL DEVICE = 0A80

DB2 EVIDENCE:
  D9G1MSTR
  D9G1IRLM
  D9GDBM1
  D9GDIST
  DB2I primary option menu accessible

CICS EVIDENCE:
  CICSA visible
  WELCOME TO CICS screen accessible

LESSON:
  Do not repeat cold-start IPL sequences when the system is already in the desired DB profile.
  Verify first, then document.
