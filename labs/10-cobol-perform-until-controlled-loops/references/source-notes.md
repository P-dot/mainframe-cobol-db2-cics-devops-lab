# Source notes

This lab is based on the user's COBOL loop practice performed on the ADCD environment.

Covered topic:

- COBOL paragraph repetition with `PERFORM ... UNTIL`
- Numeric counters using `PIC 9`
- Counter initialization with `MOVE`
- Counter increment with `ADD`
- SYSIN/SYSOUT batch validation
- Operational risk of uncontrolled loops
