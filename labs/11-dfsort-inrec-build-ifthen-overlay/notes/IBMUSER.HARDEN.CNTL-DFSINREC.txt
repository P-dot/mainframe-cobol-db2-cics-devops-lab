LAB 11 - DFSORT INREC BUILD AND CONDITIONAL OVERLAY

OBJECTIVE:
  Use DFSORT to transform customer records without changing the input member.

INPUT:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLIENTE1)

OUTPUT:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLIINR)
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLIFLG)

JCL:
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(INREC1)
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(INREC2)

DFSORT CONTROL STATEMENTS:
  SORT FIELDS=(1,7,ZD,A)
  INREC BUILD=(1,7,C' ',8,12,C'-$-',36,8,C' ',28,7,C'-',DATE1)

  SORT FIELDS=(1,7,ZD,A)
  INREC IFTHEN=(WHEN=(36,8,ZD,LT,400),OVERLAY=(47:C'MENOR')),
        IFTHEN=(WHEN=NONE,OVERLAY=(47:C'MAYOR'))

VALIDATION:
  CLIINR shows reformatted records with date.
  CLIFLG marks records as MENOR/MAYOR depending on amount.

LESSON:
  DFSORT can perform batch transformation and conditional annotation without COBOL.
