LAB 09 - DFSORT RECORD SELECTION

OBJECTIVE:
  Use DFSORT to select or omit records based on amount fields and format output records.

INPUT:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLIENTE1)

OUTPUT:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLI600)
  IBMUSER.COBOL.FILE80(CLI399)

JCL:
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(SEL600)
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(OMT400)

SEL600:
  SORT FIELDS=COPY
  INCLUDE COND=(36,8,CH,LT,C'00000600')
  OUTREC FIELDS=(DATE1,C' ',8,12,C' $',36,8)

OMT400:
  SORT FIELDS=COPY
  OMIT COND=(36,8,CH,GE,C'00000400')
  OUTREC FIELDS=(DATE,C' ',8,12,C' $',36,8)

LESSON:
  INCLUDE keeps records that match the condition.
  OMIT excludes records that match the condition.
  OUTREC builds a new output layout from selected fields and constants.
