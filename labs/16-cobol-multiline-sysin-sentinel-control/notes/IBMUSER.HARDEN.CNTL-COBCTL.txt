CLASE 21 - COBOL MULTI-LINE SYSIN WITH SENTINEL CONTROL

SOURCE:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.SRC(PARMCTL)

JCL:
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(CTLALL)

KEY PATTERN:
  01 W-ENTRADA.
     05 W-CTRL  PIC X.
     05 W-TEXTO PIC X(50).

  PERFORM 2000-PROCESO UNTIL W-CTRL = '%'.

VALIDATION:
  CTLALL ended with COND CODE 0000.
  SYSOUT displayed three message lines and did not print the sentinel.
