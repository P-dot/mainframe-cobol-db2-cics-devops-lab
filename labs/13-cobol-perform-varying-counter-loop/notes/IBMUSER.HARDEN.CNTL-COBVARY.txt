CLASE 18 - COBOL PERFORM VARYING

OBJECTIVE:
  Build and run a COBOL batch program using PERFORM VARYING.

SOURCE:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.SRC(PARMVRY)

JCL:
  IBMUSER.ML.JCL(VRYALL)

LOAD:
  IBMUSER.COBOL.LOAD(PARMVRY)

KEY COBOL:
  PERFORM 2000-PROCESO
     VARYING W-CONTADOR FROM 1 BY 1
     UNTIL W-CONTADOR = 6.

LESSON:
  PERFORM VARYING embeds the counter initial value, increment and stop condition
  inside the PERFORM statement.

VALIDATION:
  SYSOUT displayed one initialization line and five repeated message lines.
